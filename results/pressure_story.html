The Scale Story

The 2B model is nearly twice as honest under calm conditions (15/20 vs 8/20). But under pressure, honesty craters to just 2/20 — a 87% drop. The 0.8B was already less honest at baseline, and pressure took it to zero. More capacity means more honesty to lose.

The Breakthrough on 2B

On the 2B model, causal steering works in the expected direction. Injecting the pressure vector increases shortcut probability (51.8% → 58.7%). Injecting the calm vector decreases it (51.8% → 44.8%). This is a directionally correct causal effect — the internal geometry of honesty vs. shortcuts is more interpretable in the bigger model.

The Finding

Pressure Scales — But So Does Resistance

Evaluator pressure creates a measurable internal signature in both Qwen 3.5 0.8B and 2B, and shifts both models away from honesty toward shortcut reasoning. But the comparison reveals something richer:

  • The 2B model is more honest at baseline (75% vs 40%) — more capacity, more principled
  • Pressure still devastates both — 2B drops from 75% to 10% honest, 0.8B from 40% to 0%
  • Both peak at layer 23 — the pressure signature lives in the same architectural location
  • The 0.8B spike is sharper (34.2 vs 18.2 separation) — pressure carves deeper in the smaller model
  • Causal steering works on 2B — the pressure vector moves shortcut probability in the correct direction, unlike on 0.8B where it was real but non-causal
